# Amanoi Wellness & Spa — Policies & Etiquette

## 1. Booking & Reservation Policy
* **Advance Reservation**: Advance booking is strongly recommended due to high demand for private hydrotherapy suites and lakeside treatment pavilions.
* **Consultation Window**: First-time guests are required to complete an extensive holistic health consultation prior to treatment.
* **Booking Channels**: Directly coordinated via the guest's Villa Host / Butler, through the Spa Concierge (`amanoi.res@aman.com`), or telephone `+84 (0) 259 377 0777`.

## 2. Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
* **Minimum Notice Window**: A minimum of **6 hours notice** is strictly required for cancellations or appointment modifications for individual spa treatments.
* **Cancellation Charges**:
  * Cancellations made with **less than 6 hours notice** will be billed at **100% of the full treatment fee**.
  * No-shows without prior cancellation are charged at **100% full fee**.
* **Spa House Programmes & Immersions**: Private Wellness Pool Villa bookings and multi-day retreats require at least 24 hours advance cancellation notice.

## 3. Arrival & Spa Etiquette
* **Arrival Advisory**: Guests should arrive at the spa reception at least **10 to 15 minutes before** their scheduled appointment to complete documentation, enjoy welcome herbal teas, and begin mental unwinding.
* **Buggy Transfers**: Complimentary private electric buggy pick-up from guest pavilions is scheduled **15 minutes prior** to the booking time.
* **Meals & Alcohol**: Guests are advised to avoid heavy meals and alcoholic drinks for at least 90 minutes before any massage or hydrotherapy session.
* **Sanctuary of Silence**: To maintain an atmosphere of profound tranquility, guests are requested to keep voices low and switch mobile phones and electronic devices to silent mode.
* **Spa Attire & Modesty**: Robes, slippers, and disposable undergarments are provided. Therapists practice strict, respectful professional draping at all times.
* **Valuables**: High-value jewelry and electronics should be kept in villa safety boxes. The spa assumes no responsibility for lost personal belongings.

## 4. Health, Medical & Pregnancy Policy
* **Holistic Consultation Form**: A comprehensive pre-treatment health questionnaire must be completed and signed prior to the first session, detailing recent surgeries, spinal issues, allergies, or chronic conditions.
* **Pregnancy Protocol**:
  * Body treatments are not performed during the **first trimester (under 12 weeks)**.
  * During the 2nd and 3rd trimesters, certified prenatal therapists offer tailored maternal bodywork and facials using specialized body-support cushioning. Contrast hydrotherapy, thermal banya, hammam, and high-intensity abdominal massage are strictly prohibited for expectant mothers.

## 5. Age Policy
* **Adult Facilities**: Hydrotherapy suites, private Spa Houses (Banya and Hammam), and the gymnasium are restricted to guests aged **16 years and older**.
* **Younger Guests**: Tailored gentle treatments, nail grooming, and family mindfulness sessions can be organized for children and adolescents upon advance consultation with the spa director.

## 6. Payment Terms & Gratuity
* **Billing**: Incurred charges are quoted in Vietnamese Dong (VND) or converted to USD, subject to a 5% service charge and applicable statutory VAT (10%).
* **Settlement**: Posted directly to the guest pavilion room folio or settled via major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, JCB).
* **Gratuities**: Service charge is included; discretionary tips for therapists are welcomed.
