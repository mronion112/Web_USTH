# Amanoi Wellness & Spa — Facilities & Operations

## 1. Exclusive Wellness Pool Villas (Private Spa Houses)
Amanoi features two standalone, self-contained **Wellness Pool Villas** designed for guests seeking completely private spa retreats:

### 1. An Son Wellness Villa (Forest Banya Villa)
* **Setting**: Hidden within the tranquil forested hillside of Núi Chúa National Park.
* **Thermal Features**:
  * Authentic timber **Russian Banya** with wood-burning thermal stove.
  * Cold plunge pool for contrast cold-water therapy.
  * Outdoor heated Jacuzzi and specialized ice fountain.
* **Treatment Amenities**: Double spa treatment room, private 15-meter swimming pool, expansive outdoor sun deck, and dedicated steam shower.
* **Signature Ritual**: The traditional *venik* ritual (rhythmic thermal steaming using bundles of oak or eucalyptus branches) combined with ice therapy to awaken deep microcirculation.

### 2. Thuy Lien Wellness Villa (Lake Hammam Villa)
* **Setting**: Positioned along the edge of the Lotus Lake with uninterrupted aquatic views.
* **Thermal Features**:
  * Authentic domed marble **Hammam** steam house inspired by Moroccan and Ottoman traditions.
  * Heated marble treatment slab (*göbek taşı*).
  * Cold plunge pool, outdoor heated Jacuzzi, and ice fountain.
* **Treatment Amenities**: Double treatment suite, private 15-meter plunge pool, and lakefront relaxation pavilion.
* **Signature Ritual**: Deep thermal steaming, black soap body exfoliation using traditional *kessa* gloves, and purifying rhassoul volcanic mud wraps.

## 2. Spa Pavilions & Hydrotherapy Infrastructure
* **Lakeside Treatment Suites**: 5 spacious double treatment pavilions built along Lotus Lake, featuring floor-to-ceiling glass doors opening to private outdoor timber terraces, outdoor soaking bathtubs, and en-suite dressing quarters.
* **Dedicated Hydrotherapy Suites**: 2 private hydrothermal suites each equipped with a heated stone treatment table, steam room, Jacuzzi, cold plunge pool, and open-air relaxation deck.
* **Finishing Salon**: Dedicated beauty and grooming sanctuary for hair styling, luxury manicures, and pedicures.

## 3. Movement, Mindfulness & Fitness Facilities
* **Floating Yoga Pavilion**: An open-air wooden pavilion resting directly on the lotus lake, surrounded by pink lotus blooms and mountain reflections; hosts daily sunrise yoga, breathwork, and crystal singing bowl sound healing.
* **Pilates Studio**: Glass-enclosed, sunlit studio fitted with balanced-body Allegro Reformers, cadillacs, and core conditioning apparatus.
* **Gymnasium**: Modern fitness center with panoramic lake views, Technogym cardiovascular treadmills and ellipticals, free weights, and functional strength training cages.
* **Tennis Courts**: 2 floodlit synthetic turf tennis courts nestled amidst the coastal granite rocks.
