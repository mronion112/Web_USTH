# Amanoi Wellness & Spa — Overview

## 1. Brand Concept & Setting
Amanoi is perched along the dramatic coastal cliffs of Núi Chúa National Park (a UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve) overlooking the emerald waters of Vĩnh Hy Bay, Ninh Hải District, Ninh Thuận Province, Vietnam. 

Designed by legendary architect Kerry Hill, the resort blends contemporary minimalist elegance with traditional Vietnamese architectural forms. At its spiritual heart lies the **Aman Spa**, nestled beside a serene, lotus-filled lake surrounded by towering granite boulders and indigenous coastal dry forest.

## 2. Wellness Philosophy
* **Holistic Living & Mind-Body Unity**: Grounded in the belief that true wellness unites mind, body, and spirit as one indissoluble whole. Amanoi integrates time-honored Vietnamese and Eastern healing modalities (acupressure, cupping, herbalism, visceral energy work) with modern clinical skincare, contrast hydrotherapy, and mindful movement.
* **Three Global Signature Pathways**: Treatments are organized around Aman's core healing pillars:
  1. *Grounding*: For reconnecting, calming overstimulation, and centering emotional stability.
  2. *Purifying*: For deep cellular detoxification, lymphatic clearing, and energetic cleansing.
  3. *Nourishing*: For restoring depleted vitality, emotional healing, and cellular rejuvenation.
* **Hydrotherapy as Medicine**: Amanoi pioneered the luxury "Spa House" concept in Southeast Asia, elevating thermal bathing, Russian *banya*, and Moroccan *hammam* into transformative daily wellness rituals.
