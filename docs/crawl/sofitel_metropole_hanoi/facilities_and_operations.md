# Le Spa du Metropole — Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi — Facilities & Operations

## 1. Spatial Layout & Architecture
* **Total Area**: 400 square meters distributed over several levels, seamlessly integrated into the historic colonial architecture of the Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi.
* **Views & Ambience**: Overlooks the tranquil inner garden courtyard, historic French colonial balconies, and the outdoor hotel swimming pool.
* **Treatment Suites Configuration**:
  * **2 Thematic Spa Suites for Couples**: Opulent double suites with private dressing areas, couple massage tables, relaxation lounge, and en-suite **hydrotherapy soaking tubs**.
  * **6 Individual Private Spa Suites**: Uniquely styled private suites offering complete acoustic isolation and tailored lighting for individual therapies.

## 2. Hydrothermal & Wet Facilities
* **Herbal Steam Room**: Infused with native Vietnamese lemongrass, peppermint, and eucalyptus leaves for pre-treatment thermal relaxation and opening pores.
* **Finnish Dry Sauna**: High-temperature dry pine sauna promoting cardiovascular circulation and muscular relaxation.
* **Private Hydrotherapy Soaking Tubs**: Integrated into the couple suites, utilized for bespoke floral petal, milk, and aromatherapy baths.

## 3. Salon & Beauty Stations
* **Manicure & Pedicure Booth**: Ergonomic luxury beauty station equipped for professional nail shaping, cuticle nourishment, and gel finishing.
* **Relaxation Lounge**: Serene reception and post-treatment waiting salon where guests enjoy signature herbal ginger tea and dried lotus seeds.

## 4. Product Lines & Cosmetic Partners
* **Sothys (Paris)**: World-renowned French luxury institute cosmetic house. Features proprietary *Digi-Esthétique* acupressure massage protocols and targeted cosmeceuticals for cellular anti-aging, youth restoration, and multi-layer hyaluronic hydration.
* **Anne Semonin (Paris)**: Pioneer of personalized French Haute Beauté skincare, utilizing tailored botanical serums and trace element complexes formulated to address specific skin imbalances.
* **Authentic Vietnamese Indigenous Botanicals**:
  * Finely crushed Northern Vietnamese rice grains (*Gạo xay*) for gentle full-body exfoliation.
  * Fresh ginger root (*Gừng già*) and lemongrass (*Sả chanh*) for stimulating circulation.
  * Cold-pressed virgin coconut oil (*Dầu dừa*) and unrefined sea salts.
  * Pure amber resin (*Hổ phách*) and myrrh (*Một dược*) for exotic sensual body wraps.
