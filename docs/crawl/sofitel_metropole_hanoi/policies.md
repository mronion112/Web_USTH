# Le Spa du Metropole — Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi — Policies & Etiquette

## 1. Booking & Reservation Policy
* **Advance Reservation**: Prior booking is strongly encouraged, especially for the 2 private couples suites and late afternoon/evening peak hours (16:00 – 20:30).
* **Last Appointment Window**: The last appointment session starts at **20:30** (closing at 22:00).
* **Booking Channels**: Directly via internal hotel phone (`Ext: 8700`), email at `metropolehanoi.spa@sofitel.com`, or through the hotel concierge. Non-hotel residents may be asked for credit card details to secure the booking.

## 2. Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
* **Notice Period**: A minimum of **4 to 6 hours notice** is required to cancel or reschedule an appointment without penalty.
* **Late Cancellation Charge**: Cancellations made with less than 4 hours notice incur a **50% cancellation fee**.
* **No-Show Charge**: Failure to arrive for a scheduled appointment without prior notice will result in a **100% full charge** billed to the guest room folio or credit card.
* **Couple Suites & Signature Packages**: Cancellations for private suite experiences require at least 12 hours advance notice.

## 3. Arrival & Spa Etiquette
* **Arrival Time**: Guests are requested to arrive at the spa reception **15 to 20 minutes prior** to the scheduled appointment time. This allows time to check in, complete the consultation questionnaire, change into robes and slippers, and enjoy welcome tea.
* **Late Arrival**: Arriving late will result in a curtailed treatment duration so that following guest appointments are not disrupted. The full service fee remains payable.
* **Serenity & Electronic Devices**: To preserve the peaceful atmosphere, guests must ensure mobile phones and electronic devices are turned off or kept on silent mode.
* **Attire & Discretion**: Disposable underwear is provided for body scrubs, wraps, and massage services. Therapists ensure professional draping to maintain complete guest modesty.
* **Valuables**: Lockers are available inside treatment suites; however, guests are advised not to bring jewelry or large amounts of cash. The spa is not responsible for lost personal items.
* **Smoking & Alcohol**: Smoking, electronic cigarettes, and alcohol consumption are strictly prohibited inside the spa suites and wet facilities.

## 4. Health, Medical & Pregnancy Policy
* **Health Consultation Profile**: Guests must complete a confidential pre-treatment health questionnaire detailing conditions such as hypertension, heart ailments, recent surgeries, physical injuries, or skin sensitivities.
* **Pregnancy**:
  * Body massages and thermal heat treatments are strictly prohibited during the **first trimester (under 12 weeks)**.
  * For guests in their second or third trimesters, certified therapists provide gentle maternal massages and hydrating facials using side-lying pillows and neutral botanical oils. Deep tissue work, hot stone therapy, and hydrotherapy tubs are contraindicated.

## 5. Age Restrictions
* **Adult Sanctuary**: The spa facilities, sauna, steam rooms, and adult body therapies are reserved for guests aged **16 years and older**.
* **Junior Guests**: Guests aged 12 to 15 may enjoy selected express manicures, pedicures, or gentle facial treatments when accompanied by an adult in a private suite.

## 6. Payment Terms & Gratuity
* **Taxes & Surcharges**: All prices are quoted in Vietnamese Dong (VND) and subject to a **5% service charge** and **statutory government VAT** (8%–10%).
* **Payment Methods**: Direct billing to the hotel room folio, major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, JCB), and cash.
* **Gratuities**: Service charge is included in the final bill; additional tips for exceptional service are at the guest's discretion.
