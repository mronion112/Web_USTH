# Le Spa du Metropole — Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi — Overview

## 1. Brand Concept & Heritage
Le Spa du Metropole is the luxury wellness enclave of the iconic Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi, established in 1901 and located in the historic heart of Hanoi's French Quarter.

The spa represents the elegant confluence of French *art de vivre* (the refined art of living) and timeless Vietnamese aesthetic grace. Spanning 400 square meters over multiple levels, the facility overlooks the hotel's lush interior garden courtyard and outdoor swimming pool.

## 2. Wellness Philosophy
* **East Meets West Haute Beauté**: Ancient Vietnamese herbal traditions and meridian balancing therapies converge seamlessly with Parisian Haute Beauté cosmetic science.
* **Exclusive French Brand Partnerships**: Collaborating with France's most prestigious luxury cosmetic houses—**Sothys (Paris)** and **Anne Semonin (Paris)**—delivering customized Digi-Esthétique facial protocols and personalized trace element serums.
* **Signature Sensory Journey**: Each guest room and spa suite is infused with bespoke ambient Indochine aromas, complemented by curated French and traditional acoustic soundscapes designed to quiet the mind.
