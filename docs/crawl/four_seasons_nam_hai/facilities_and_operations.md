# Four Seasons The Nam Hai — The Heart of the Earth Spa — Facilities & Operations

## 1. Architecture & Design
* **Master Architect**: Conceived by renowned French architect **Reda Amalou** and studio **AW² Architecture**.
* **Architectural Concept**: Inspired by traditional Vietnamese garden homes (*Nhà Vườn*) of Hue and Central Vietnam, integrating Feng Shui water alignments and natural ventilation.
* **Natural Setting**: An expansive, serene freshwater lagoon teeming with blooming pink and white lotus flowers and water lilies, shaded by whispering bamboo groves and tropical palms.

## 2. 8 Floating Overwater Treatment Pavilions
* **Overwater Construction**: 8 detached, private luxury pavilions constructed directly over the lagoon, creating the sensory impression of floating on water.
* **Pavilion Features**:
  * Full glass folding walls providing panoramic views across the lotus lagoon.
  * Dedicated changing area, private vanity, and luxury bathroom.
  * **Sunken Outdoor/Overwater Bathtub**: Positioned right at the water's edge, surrounded by lotus blossoms for ritual floral soaking.
  * **Open-Air Rain Shower**: Private courtyard rain showers surrounded by lush foliage.
  * Dual ergonomic massage tables suitable for individual or synchronized couple treatments.
  * Private timber relaxation terrace over the water for post-treatment herbal tea.

## 3. Acoustic Crystal Singing Bowls (432 Hz)
* Every single treatment pavilion features a dedicated set of pure tuned quartz crystal singing bowls calibrated to **432 Hz**.
* Staff therapists are specially trained in vibrational sound acoustics to harmonize the room's energy field before touching the guest's body and to gently awaken the senses at the conclusion of every therapy.

## 4. Hydrothermal & Wet Amenities
* **Herbal Steam Rooms**: Separate male and female steam chambers infused with wild local lemongrass, eucalyptus, and mint leaves.
* **Finnish Dry Saunas**: Natural pine saunas providing deep thermal detox and muscle relaxation.
* **Cold Refreshment Showers & Ice Fountains**: For contrast thermal therapy.
* **Open-Air Lagoon Relaxation Deck**: Shaded central pavilion on the water where guests enjoy warm ginger tea, candied lotus seeds, and dried local fruit.

## 5. Movement & Fitness Facilities
* **Overwater Yoga & Meditation Pavilion**: Open-sided wooden pavilion overlooking the lagoon, hosting daily sunrise Hatha yoga, Yin yoga, pranayama, and singing bowl sound healing.
* **24/7 Fitness Centre**: State-of-the-art gym equipped with Technogym cardiovascular machinery, strength training stations, free weights, and stretching areas.
* **Outdoor Sports**: 4 tennis courts, badminton court, and basketball half-court nestled among the resort gardens.

## 6. Organic Product Lines & Ingredients
* **100% Pure, Organic & Ethical Philosophy**: Free of parabens, sodium lauryl sulfate, synthetic fragrances, petrochemicals, and animal testing.
* **Indigenous Vietnamese Ingredients**:
  * *Trầm Hương (Vietnamese Agarwood)*: Sourced sustainably from Central Vietnam for sacred grounding and smoke purification.
  * *Lotus Blossom & Seed Extract*: High in natural antioxidants, flavonoids, and soothing emollients.
  * *Highland Robusta Coffee*: Central Highlands coffee beans for microcirculation and skin exfoliation.
  * *Local Central Coast Sea Salt*: Unrefined, solar-evaporated mineral sea salt.
  * *Virgin Coconut Oil*: Cold-pressed organic coconut oil from Ben Tre and Central Vietnam.
  * *Wild Ginger, Cajuput & Lemongrass*: Freshly harvested botanicals used in poultices and steam rooms.
* **Luxury Clinical Skincare Formulations**: High-performance organic formulations enriched with 24k gold, bio-identical peptides, freshwater pearl micronutrients, and marine algae.
