# Four Seasons The Nam Hai — The Heart of the Earth Spa — Policies & Etiquette

## 1. Booking & Reservation Policy
* **Advance Reservation**: Prior booking is strongly recommended for all services, particularly for the 8 overwater pavilions, sunset slots (16:30 – 19:30), and extended multi-hour rituals.
* **Booking Channels**:
  * Resort Guests: Through the Four Seasons Mobile App, Four Seasons Chat, Resort Concierge, or In-Villa Telephone (Ext: 4).
  * Non-Resident / Day Guests: Welcome subject to availability; a valid credit card guarantee is required at the time of reservation.
* **Consultation Window**: First-time guests are requested to arrive early to complete the lifestyle and health consultation form.

## 2. Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
* **Standard Cancellation Window**: A minimum of **6 hours notice** is required for individual treatment cancellations or modifications without fee.
* **Late Cancellation Penalty**:
  * Cancellations made between **2 to 6 hours** prior to appointment time will incur a **50% cancellation fee**.
* **Last-Minute & No-Show Penalty**:
  * Cancellations made with **less than 2 hours notice** or failure to arrive (No-Show) are billed at **100% of the full treatment rate** to the room folio or guaranteed credit card.
* **Multi-Day Retreats & Group Bookings**: Require a minimum of **24 hours advance notice** to cancel without penalty.

## 3. Arrival Time & Spa Etiquette
* **Arrival Advisory**: Guests are kindly requested to arrive at the spa reception **15 to 30 minutes prior** to the scheduled appointment time. This allows time to check in, savor a welcoming herbal elixir, change into robes and slippers, and unwind.
* **Late Arrival**: Arriving late will result in a curtailed treatment duration so that subsequent guests are not delayed. The full treatment rate remains applicable.
* **Sanctuary of Tranquility**: Guests are requested to speak in hushed, soft tones throughout the spa sanctuary.
* **Digital Detachment**: To preserve the meditative atmosphere, mobile phones and electronic devices must be silenced or turned off. Photography and videography are strictly prohibited inside treatment pavilions and hydrothermal facilities.
* **Valuables**: Lockers with electronic locks are provided in pavilions, but guests are advised to store valuable jewelry, watches, and cash inside their villa room safe. The resort accepts no liability for lost items.
* **Smoking & Alcohol**: Smoking, vaping, and the consumption of alcoholic beverages are strictly forbidden inside all spa pavilions, waiting lounges, and hydrothermal facilities.

## 4. Health, Medical & Pregnancy Advisory
* **Confidential Intake Form**: Completed prior to every initial treatment. Discloses any medical conditions including cardiovascular issues, hypertension, hypotension, recent surgical procedures, skin conditions, open wounds, or joint disorders.
* **Pregnancy Policy**:
  * **First Trimester (Weeks 1 to 12)**: Body massages, wraps, and deep thermal treatments are strictly restricted for the safety of mother and child.
  * **Second & Third Trimesters (Weeks 13+)**: Expectant mothers are welcomed for the dedicated *Mother's Embrace* prenatal massage and gentle facials.
  * **Contraindicated During Pregnancy**: Saunas, steam rooms, deep-tissue abdominal pressure, and hot stone applications are strictly avoided.
* **Allergies**: Guests with allergies to nuts, essential oils, or seafood/marine products must alert the spa reception during intake so therapists can calibrate hypoallergenic carrier oils (e.g., pure grape seed or jojoba).

## 5. Children & Adolescent Policy ("Young Earth")
* **Adult Environment**: Main hydrotherapy amenities (steam, sauna) and standard adult treatments are reserved for guests aged **16 years and older**.
* **Young Earth Treatments (Ages 6 to 15)**: Children and teenagers may receive treatments exclusively from the *Young Earth* menu, provided they are accompanied by a parent or legal guardian inside a double treatment pavilion throughout the service.

## 6. Payment Terms & Gratuity
* **Currency**: All published rates are in Vietnamese Dong (VND), subject to 5% service charge and applicable statutory VAT.
* **Payment Methods**: Direct billing to the guest villa room folio, major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, JCB), and cash (VND).
* **Gratuities**: Service charge is included; discretionary tips for therapists are welcomed.
