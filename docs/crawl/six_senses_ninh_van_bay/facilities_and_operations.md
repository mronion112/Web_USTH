# Six Senses Ninh Van Bay Spa — Facilities & Operations

## 1. Spa Architecture & Pavilion Layout
* **Setting**: Tucked away in a secluded hillside enclave surrounded by lush jungle, cascading natural water features, and giant granite rock formations.
* **Treatment Rooms**:
  * **6 Private Double Treatment Rooms**: Equipped with individual changing areas, outdoor showers, and floor-to-ceiling glass windows framing rock waterfalls and tropical greenery.
  * **1 Family Spa Suite**: Specially sized suite accommodating couples or families receiving concurrent gentle treatments.
  * **Outdoor Relaxation Terraces**: Shaded open-air timber decks overlooking the bay and jungle where herbal teas and infused waters are served post-treatment.

## 2. Hydrothermal & Wet Amenities
* **Herbal Steam Rooms**: Separate male and female cedar steam chambers infused with fresh lemongrass and eucalyptus botanicals.
* **Finnish Saunas**: Traditional dry-heat timber saunas for deep cardiovascular stimulation and detoxification.
* **Natural Stone Herbal Soaking Tubs**: Carved volcanic stone and timber bathtubs for customized milk, floral, and medicinal herbal soaking rituals.
* **Open-Air Rain Showers**: Outdoor showers embedded into granite rocks surrounded by tropical foliage.

## 3. Dedicated Movement & Specialist Spaces
* **Interactive Alchemy Bar**: A dedicated apothecary island featuring stone pestles, mortars, glass jars of dried and fresh herbs, Himalayan salts, and essential oils where guests craft customized scrubs.
* **Biohacking Lounge**: Equipped with modern recovery technology including Normatec dynamic pneumatic compression boots, Hypervolt vibration percussion guns, and Venom thermal back wraps.
* **Wellness Screening Consultation Suite**: Private diagnostic consultation room fitted with bio-impedance body composition analyzers, pulse oximetry, and stress-biomarker monitoring equipment.
* **Yoga & Meditation Shala**: Open-air timber pavilion set high on the hillside offering panoramic views of the bay, equipped with aerial yoga hammocks, meditation cushions, and singing bowls.
* **24/7 Fitness Center (Gym)**: Fully air-conditioned fitness studio equipped with Technogym cardiovascular runners, stationary bikes, elliptical trainers, free weights, and functional training stations.
* **Jungle Gym Obstacle Trail**: An innovative outdoor exercise trail integrating natural logs, climbing ropes, and stone lifting stations into the forest landscape.
* **Beauty & Grooming Salon**: Dedicated salon stations for luxury manicures, pedicures, and nail care.

## 4. Products & Brand Partnerships
* **Farm-to-Spa Botanicals**: Fresh herbs (lemongrass, ginger, Vietnamese mint, betel leaf, mugwort) harvested directly from the resort's private organic garden.
* **Indigenous Vietnamese Ingredients**:
  * *Cốm Xanh* (young fragrant green rice from Northern Vietnam) used in signature body polishes.
  * Cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil sourced from Khanh Hoa and Ben Tre.
  * Raw organic honey and sea salt harvested from local coastal salt flats.
  * Highland green coffee bean extract for microcirculation and anti-cellulite therapy.
* **Professional Spa Cosmetics**:
  * High-grade natural essential oils free of synthetic preservatives, parabens, and artificial fragrances.
  * Clinical marine extracts, spirulina algae, and sea buckthorn oil for anti-aging and facial therapies.
