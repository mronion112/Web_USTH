# Six Senses Ninh Van Bay Spa — Policies & Etiquette

## 1. Booking & Reservation Policy
* **Advance Booking**: Advance reservations are strongly advised, especially for late-afternoon time slots (15:00 – 19:00), multi-hour signature rituals, and couples therapies.
* **Booking Channels**: Appointments may be booked through the guest's dedicated GEM (Guest Experience Maker), directly at the Spa Reception desk, or by contacting `reservations-ninhvan-spa@sixsenses.com`.
* **Consultation Window**: First-time guests are required to confirm their booking with sufficient time to complete health intake profiling.

## 2. Cancellation & Modification Policy
* **Cancellation Notice Window**: A minimum of **4 to 6 hours notice** is required for any cancellation or rescheduling of individual treatments.
* **Late Cancellation Charges**:
  * Cancellations made less than 4 hours prior to the scheduled appointment are charged a **50% cancellation fee**.
* **No-Show Policy**:
  * Failure to show up without prior notice results in a **100% full charge** billed to the guest's villa folio.
* **Multi-Day Programs**: Package or retreat program cancellations require at least 24 hours advance notice to avoid penalties.

## 3. Arrival & Spa Etiquette
* **Recommended Arrival Time**: Guests are kindly requested to arrive **15 to 30 minutes prior** to the scheduled appointment time. This allows time to complete the health questionnaire, change into provided robes and slippers, and unwind in the herbal steam or sauna facilities.
* **Late Arrival Penalty**: Arriving late will result in a shortened treatment duration so that following guest appointments are not disrupted. The full treatment rate will still apply.
* **Sanctuary of Tranquility**: The spa is a space of peace and quiet. Guests are asked to maintain a low speaking tone and keep mobile phones and smart devices turned off or switched to silent mode. Photography is strictly prohibited in public treatment and wet facility areas.
* **Valuables & Jewelry**: Guests are encouraged to leave valuable jewelry and personal electronics inside their villa safety deposit box. The spa provides personal lockers for robes and sandals but accepts no liability for lost valuables.
* **Smoking & Alcohol**: Smoking, vaping, and the consumption of alcoholic beverages are strictly forbidden within all spa pavilions and hydrothermal facilities.

## 4. Health, Medical & Pregnancy Policy
* **Pre-Treatment Consultation Form**: Every guest must complete a confidential wellness intake questionnaire before commencing therapy, disclosing any conditions such as high/low blood pressure, cardiovascular disorders, recent surgeries, physical injuries, or allergies.
* **Pregnancy Guidelines**:
  * Expectant mothers must inform the spa team upon reservation.
  * Body therapies are strictly restricted during the **first trimester (weeks 1 to 12)** for safety reasons.
  * From the **second trimester onward**, specialized Prenatal Massages and gentle botanical facials are available using customized body support pillows and pregnancy-safe organic oils. Deep tissue techniques, abdominal work, and heat therapies (steam, sauna, hot stone) are strictly avoided.
* **Allergies & Skin Conditions**: Guests with sensitive skin or nut/botanical allergies must notify the therapist so that custom essential oil blends or neutral cold-pressed carrier oils (e.g., pure grape seed or jojoba) can be substituted at the Alchemy Bar.

## 5. Age Policy
* **General Age Restriction**: The spa treatment menu, wet facilities (steam and sauna), and gym are reserved for guests aged **16 years and older**.
* **Junior & Teen Treatments**: Select gentle manicures, pedicures, and light relaxation massages may be arranged for younger guests (under 16) in designated family treatment rooms, provided they are accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.

## 6. Payment & Gratuities
* **Pricing Currency**: All published rates are in Vietnamese Dong (VND) and inclusive of 5% service charge and applicable statutory VAT.
* **Payment Methods**: Accepted methods include direct billing to the guest villa room folio, major international credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, JCB), and cash (VND).
* **Gratuities**: Service charge is included in all bills; additional gratuities for therapists are voluntary and at the guest’s discretion.
