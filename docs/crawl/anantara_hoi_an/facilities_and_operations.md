# Anantara Hoi An Spa — Facilities & Operations

## 1. Spatial Layout & Architecture
* **Architectural Style**: French colonial low-rise pavilion architecture with clay-tiled roofs, louvered wooden shutters, and shaded verandas overlooking tropical courtyard gardens and the Thu Bon River.
* **Treatment Rooms**:
  * **Couples Treatment Suites**: Dedicated double suites equipped with private **sunken terrazzo bathtubs**, en-suite rain showers, and relaxation daybeds.
  * **Single Treatment Rooms**: Secluded, sound-insulated private therapy rooms for individual treatments.
  * **Foot Reflexology Lounge**: Comfortable reclining armchairs with individual warm herbal foot-soaking basins for targeted foot therapy.

## 2. Hydrothermal & Relaxation Amenities
* **Sunken Soaking Bathtubs**: Built-in sunken stone baths within couples suites designed for bespoke floral and herbal soaking rituals.
* **Herbal Steam Facilities**: Dedicated steam chamber infused with local lemongrass, eucalyptus, and mint leaves for pre-massage muscle loosening.
* **Riverfront Yoga & Meditation Deck**: Open-air timber pavilion set right beside the Thu Bon River, surrounded by tropical foliage, utilized for morning Hatha/Vinyasa yoga and evening sound healing sessions.
* **Post-Treatment Relaxation Garden**: Shaded outdoor terrace with water fountains and comfortable loungers where herbal ginger tea and local candied lotus seeds are served.

## 3. Product Lines & Healing Elements
* **Himalayan Salt Therapy**: Heated natural pink Himalayan crystal bricks used directly for skin remineralization and therapeutic heat transfer.
* **Singing Bowls**: Hand-hammered Himalayan singing bowls tuned to therapeutic vibrational frequencies for sound baths and chakra clearing.
* **Handcrafted Herbal Compresses (*Túi Chườm Thảo Dược*)**: Prepared with authentic Vietnamese medicinal herbs:
  * *Ngải cứu* (Artemisia / Mugwort) for joint ache relief and blood circulation.
  * *Gừng già* (Aged ginger) for warming meridians and dissipating cold.
  * *Sả chanh* (Lemongrass) for purification and muscular relaxation.
  * *Quế Trà Bồng* (Quang Nam cinnamon) for soothing stiffness and aroma.
* **Local Organic Exfoliants**:
  * Robusta coffee grounds from Vietnam's Central Highlands.
  * Khanh Hoa / Quang Nam unrefined sea salts.
  * Cold-pressed extra-virgin coconut oil.
* **Cosmetic Partnerships**: High-end European clinical botanical skincare (Comfort Zone) formulated with natural bio-actives, free of parabens, mineral oils, and synthetic colorants.
