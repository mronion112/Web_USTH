# Anantara Hoi An Spa — Overview

## 1. Brand Concept & Setting
Anantara Hoi An Spa is situated within the Anantara Hoi An Resort at 1 Pham Hong Thai Street, along the tranquil banks of the historic Thu Bon River in Hoi An, Quang Nam Province. Located just a short stroll from the UNESCO World Heritage Ancient Town, the spa captures the French colonial and traditional Vietnamese architectural heritage of Hoi An, surrounded by lush tropical gardens, whispering bamboo, and serene river vistas.

The spa is conceived as a tranquil riverfront refuge where timeless Southeast Asian healing traditions intersect with contemporary wellness practices.

## 2. Wellness Philosophy
* **Indigenous Roots & Herbal Wisdom**: Treatments draw deeply from centuries-old Vietnamese herbal lore, utilizing local botanicals such as lemongrass, ginger, mugwort (*ngải cứu*), cinnamon, and star anise.
* **Harmonizing Mind, Body & Soul**: The spa experience focuses on restorative balance through touch, aroma, sound, and thermal therapies. Signature therapies like the Traditional Vietnamese Herbal Experience and Sound Healing Ritual create a sanctuary for sensory renewal.
* **Personalized Journeys**: From half-day couple retreats with private sunken bathtubs to targeted therapeutic work for weary travelers exploring the ancient town on foot, services are tailored to individual energy levels.
