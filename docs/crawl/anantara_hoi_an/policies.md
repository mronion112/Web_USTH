# Anantara Hoi An Spa — Policies & Etiquette

## 1. Booking & Reservation Policy
* **Advance Reservation**: Prior reservations are strongly recommended to ensure availability of preferred treatment times, private couples suites, and therapist gender preferences.
* **Peak Hours**: Early morning and late afternoon/evening hours (15:00 – 19:30) book out fast; advance notice of at least 24 hours is advised.
* **Booking Channels**: Directly at the Spa reception, by calling `+84 235 391 4555`, or via email at `spa.ahar@anantara.com`.

## 2. Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
* **Single Treatments**: A minimum of **3 to 4 hours notice** is required for cancellation or modification without penalty.
  * Cancellations made with less than 3 hours notice will incur a **50% cancellation fee**.
  * No-shows are charged at **100% of the full treatment rate**.
* **Spa Packages & Private Suites**: A minimum of **6 hours notice** is required for cancelling multi-treatment packages or private couple suite sessions.
* **Multi-Day Wellness Retreats**: Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours prior to commencement.

## 3. Arrival & Spa Etiquette
* **Arrival Time**: Guests are advised to arrive **15 to 20 minutes before** their scheduled appointment. This allows sufficient time to fill out the confidential consultation profile, change into spa attire (robes and slippers), and enjoy a refreshing welcome herbal elixir.
* **Late Arrival**: Arriving late will result in a shortened session so as not to inconvenience subsequent guests. The full treatment price will still apply.
* **Spa Atmosphere**: The spa is a tranquil retreat. To maintain peace and serenity for all guests, mobile phones and electronic devices must be silenced or turned off.
* **Attire & Modesty**: Disposable undergarments are provided for all body scrubs, wraps, and massage therapies. Therapists practice professional draping techniques throughout the session to ensure complete modesty.
* **Valuables**: Lockers are provided; however, guests are strongly advised to leave fine jewelry, watches, and cash in their room safe. The resort cannot accept responsibility for lost personal items.
* **Smoking & Alcohol**: Smoking, electronic cigarettes, and alcohol are strictly prohibited within the spa premises. Guests who appear intoxicated will be refused service.

## 4. Health, Medical & Pregnancy Policy
* **Health Assessment**: Guests must complete a confidential pre-treatment health profile. Please notify the spa team of any health conditions, including high/low blood pressure, cardiac history, asthma, recent surgeries, physical injuries, or skin allergies.
* **Pregnancy**:
  * Body treatments and massages are strictly avoided during the **first trimester (under 12 weeks)**.
  * During the second and third trimesters, specialized pregnancy-safe massages and gentle facials are offered using neutral oils and comfortable side-lying cushioning. Thermal facilities, deep tissue techniques, and abdominal pressure are prohibited.

## 5. Age Policy
* **Adult Access**: Guests aged **16 years and older** have full independent access to spa therapies and facilities.
* **Minors & Children**: Guests under 16 years of age may enjoy selected express manicures, pedicures, or gentle massages only when accompanied by a parent or legal guardian inside a private treatment room.

## 6. Payment & Gratuity Guidelines
* **Taxes & Surcharges**: All rates are subject to 5% service charge and applicable statutory government VAT.
* **Payment Methods**: Cash (VND), all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, JCB, American Express), and direct room charge to the guest's resort folio.
* **Gratuities**: Tips for exceptional service from therapists are entirely at the guest's discretion.
